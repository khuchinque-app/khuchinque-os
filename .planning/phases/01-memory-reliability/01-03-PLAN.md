<task type="checkpoint:decision" gate="blocking">
  <decision>What archival threshold triggers log rotation?</decision>
  <context>Daily logs grow each session. Need a rule for when to archive. Options: by age (archive logs older than N days), by size (archive when daily log exceeds N KB), by session count (archive after N sessions), or a combination.</context>
  <options>
    <option id="age-based"><name>Age-based (30 days)</name><pros>Simple, predictable; old context naturally becomes less relevant</pros><cons>Active long-running sessions may archive still-relevant data</cons></option>
    <option id="size-based"><name>Size-based (50KB per log)</name><pros>Directly bounds context impact</pros><cons>Hard to predict when archival triggers; logs of same age may differ in size</cons></option>
    <option id="session-count"><name>Session-count (archive after 5 sessions)</name><pros>Aligns with session lifecycle</pros><cons>Session frequency varies; one busy day could trigger multiple archives</cons></option>
    <option id="combo"><name>Combination (30 days OR 50KB, whichever first)</name><pros>Most robust — catches both age and size issues</pros><cons>More complex to implement</cons></option>
  </options>
  <resume-signal>Select: age-based, size-based, session-count, or combo</resume-signal>
